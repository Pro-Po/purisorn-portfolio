Challenge

A specialist distributor was competing with marketplaces for the same demand.

35Yontrakan sells motorcycle spare parts through retail and wholesale channels. The website began without search visibility or domain authority, while large marketplaces dominated commercial results.

Because purchases often complete offline, the site had to do more than list inventory. It needed to become a trusted discovery and education layer that could guide customers toward the right part and the business.

My ownership

The client gave me the autonomy to build and grow the channel end to end.

Built

Developed the Webflow site from a teammate's design and created its taxonomy, URLs, CMS structure, domains, and DNS setup.

Owned

Led keyword selection, editorial roadmap, briefs, writing, editing, on-page SEO, analytics, verification, and link strategy.

Maintained

Run quarterly optimization across site speed, mobile performance, imagery, internal links, and new product or information pages.

Strategy

Move from a catalog to a knowledge system for riders.

Foundation

Build the full site and measurement layer

Created the website, sitemap, product structure, Google Analytics, Tag Manager, and Search Console foundation from the ground up.

Product discovery

Expand pages with the inventory

Maintained and expanded product and category pages as products launched, making specific parts and use cases easier to discover.

Horizontal growth

Expand from products into the rider's wider journey

Added technical Q&A, parts comparisons, licence-renewal guidance, changing terms and conditions, motorcycle laws, and practical how-to content.

Authority flow

Turn winning pages into a connected knowledge base

Built links from trusted sources and used strong internal links from winning pages to support related product and information pages.
