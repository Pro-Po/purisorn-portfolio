Challenge

The company was more than a factory, but search could not see the difference.

Thumbinthai is a Bangkok apparel manufacturer and consultant for brands developing custom clothing. Its value spans fabric development, pattern making, printing, embroidery, low-MOQ production, and export support.

The digital experience needed to translate that complex capability into clear entry points for buyers with different levels of production knowledge, while remaining useful as search behavior shifted toward AI-generated answers.

My ownership

I have owned the website and search strategy since day one of the client's digital growth.

Built

Created the Webflow experience, information architecture, targeted landing pages, on-page system, and conversion paths.

Scaled

Worked with the client to grow from one page to 100 pages and separate Thai and international sites without losing search structure.

Evolved

Expanded from product SEO into image search, brand authority, case-led content, structured Q&A, and AEO-ready expertise.

Strategy

Turn services and buyer questions into targeted search journeys.

Experience

Build a responsive path from discovery to inquiry

Created a mobile-ready website with calls to action on every page. The next lifecycle layer is a planned HubSpot connection for CRM and email marketing.

High-intent layers

Match pages to how OEM buyers narrow their needs

Mapped category and product intent such as “โรงงานผลิตเสื้อยืด,” “สั่งทำเสื้อครอป,” and “เสื้อ baby tee โรงงาน,” while optimizing imagery to compete alongside marketplace results.

Market architecture

Expand correctly across Thai and international demand

Planned separate site structures and keyword systems for local and global buyers as the business grew beyond its first site.

AEO evolution

Move from generic explanations to evidence and point of view

Adapted content from basic what/how/why articles toward real cases, brand solutions, strategic guidance, and credible answers that AI systems can reference.
