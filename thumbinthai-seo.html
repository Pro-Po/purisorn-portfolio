Challenge

The company was more than a factory, but search could not see the difference.

Thumbinthai is a Bangkok apparel manufacturer and consultant for brands developing custom clothing. Its value spans fabric development, pattern making, printing, embroidery, low-MOQ production, and export support.

The digital experience needed to translate that complex capability into clear entry points for buyers with different levels of production knowledge, while remaining useful as search behavior shifted toward AI-generated answers.

Strategy

Turn services and buyer questions into targeted search journeys.

Experience

Build a responsive path from discovery to inquiry

Created a mobile-ready website that makes production capabilities, product types, customization options, and contact paths easier to understand.

Landing pages

Match pages to specific production intent

Developed targeted pages around apparel types, private-label needs, services, and the questions buyers ask before requesting a quote.

SEO foundation

Keep relevance strong as the offer expands

Applied technical and on-page optimization so products, services, and educational content reinforce rather than compete with one another.

AEO evolution

Make expertise easier for answer engines to retrieve

Used clear service explanations and question-led content as the foundation for GEO and AEO as search results shifted toward generated answers in 2025.
