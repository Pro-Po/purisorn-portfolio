Building a Lead Engine
from Scratch

BEP Digital Agency · 2020 – Present
BEP Digital Agency — bepgroup.space
The Product

BEP Digital Agency launched in 2018 as a small digital marketing shop in Bangkok. When I joined in 2020, the company had no brand recognition, a single-page website, and a service offering too narrow to compete for meaningful clients.

The core challenge was a classic 0→1 problem: how do you build inbound demand for a company no one has heard of, with zero paid acquisition budget?

My Role

I joined as the first product & marketing hire. There was no existing process — no sales flow, no content strategy, no roadmap. I built it from the ground up while simultaneously running it.

I owned the full product lifecycle, the website roadmap, the content and SEO strategy, and the lead generation pipeline — all as a cross-functional lead.

The Problem

Leads came in. But they asked for things we couldn't deliver.

Early on, we started getting inbound inquiries — but they were the wrong fit. Our messaging was too generic and attracted clients with scope requirements that our operations couldn't handle. The result was wasted sales conversations and a team stretched thin on mismatched work.

The root issue was twofold: our service scope was too narrow to compete for larger, better-fit clients, and our messaging wasn't filtering for the right ICP. We needed to solve both at the same time.

What I Did

Expand scope. Fix the message. Build the machine.

  • Defined the ideal customer profile and rewrote service messaging to attract better-fit leads and filter out mismatches at the top of the funnel
  • Built strategic partnerships with complementary agencies to expand our service scope — allowing us to pitch and deliver on larger, more complex client needs
  • Scaled the website from 1 to 200+ pages through a structured SEO and content strategy, driving organic growth and brand discoverability without paid budget
  • Built self-serve tools including a cost calculator, 1-page SEO audit tool, and advertising mockup generator to reduce time-to-value for prospective clients and increase engagement
  • Set up the full sales pipeline — lead capture, CRM, nurture sequences, and conversion tracking — from zero
  • Continuously developed product and business strategy, identifying opportunities for product-market fit through customer feedback, A/B testing, and OKR-aligned releases
  • Deployed AI automation pipelines across the content and marketing workflow, saving 15 hours per week in production time

What I Learned

The thing I'm most proud of isn't the conversion rate — it's that we built a lead engine from nothing, without spending a single baht on ads. Setting up the operations from scratch taught me that the hardest part of 0→1 isn't the strategy — it's building the process discipline to execute it consistently while the company is still figuring itself out.
