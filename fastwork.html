The Product

Fastwork is a regional SaaS marketplace connecting freelancers and clients across Thailand and Indonesia. When I joined, the platform was web-only, conversion rates needed improvement, and the company was preparing to expand into Indonesia from scratch.

The challenge was operating on three fronts simultaneously: grow Thailand, build trust infrastructure, and launch Indonesia — all with a lean team.

My Role

Marketing manager responsible for Thailand growth and a pioneer team member for the Indonesia expansion. I owned GTM strategy, funnel optimization, and the go-to-market plan for getting to the first 1,000 customers in Indonesia within 90 days.

The Problem

Trust is everything in a two-sided marketplace — and we had a gap.

To grow both sides of the marketplace, users needed to trust the platform. That meant implementing KYC verification to reduce fraud and build confidence for both freelancers and clients. The problem: the technical system for automated verification wasn't built yet.

At the same time, we were tasked with launching in Indonesia — a new market with no brand presence, no user base, and no existing playbook. Getting to 1,000 customers in 90 days meant building trust from zero, acquiring both sides of the marketplace simultaneously, and doing it fast.

What I Did

Bridge the gap. Seed the market. Build the flywheel.

  • Designed a KYC workaround using the customer support team to manually verify users during onboarding — keeping fraud low and trust high while the technical solution was being built
  • Mapped the full inquiry-to-payment funnel across both Thailand and Indonesia markets, identified key drop-off points, and partnered with product and engineering to ship targeted fixes
  • Designed the offline acquisition strategy for Indonesia — including a booth at office buildings to simultaneously recruit freelancers and clients — and led execution through the local team
  • Launched a first-time buyer campaign that drove the first paying customer cohort in Indonesia
  • Built a unified data reference layer connecting performance marketing data to the product database, enabling end-to-end funnel visibility from campaign click through to payment and retention
  • Optimized performance marketing channels against CVR, CAC, and LTV across both markets
Indonesia Go-To-Market: 3 Fronts in 90 Days
3-track GTM strategy executed simultaneously with a lean team
What I Learned

The biggest lesson from Indonesia was that trust is the product in the early stage of a marketplace. Getting the first clients and freelancers wasn't about features — it was about showing up, being credible, and making it easy to take the first step. Once we convinced a handful of users and the product delivered, the word spread. Great product makes growth repeatable. That's the flywheel.
