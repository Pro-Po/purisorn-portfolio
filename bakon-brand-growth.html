Challenge

A retail launch needed a brand-building engine, not occasional social posts.

Bakon was entering Thailand's largest convenience-store network as a new FMCG brand. Shelf availability created an opportunity, but awareness, consideration, and repeat interest still had to be built outside the store.

With a two-person team, the challenge was to create enough consistent brand content, social distribution, and third-party credibility to support product discovery and purchase.

My ownership

My partner and I operated as the brand's full social and content team.

Created

Planned and produced still-image content and short-form video for an always-on social presence.

Distributed

Managed the brand's social channels and paid media across Meta and TikTok.

Amplified

Managed KOL relationships and content to build trust, awareness, and product purchase.

Operating system

One content system carried the brand from launch to daily demand.

Content studio

Turn products into repeatable creative formats

Produced stills and short clips that could introduce products, show consumption moments, support promotions, and give the brand a recognizable voice.

Social management

Keep the brand active across discovery channels

Managed publishing and audience-facing activity across Meta and TikTok, then used paid distribution to extend reach beyond the existing community.

KOL management

Borrow trust from relevant creators

Coordinated creator selection and content so product awareness could become social proof and a reason to purchase at 7-Eleven.

Learning loop

Use response to shape the next content cycle

Connected audience reactions, paid-social signals, creator feedback, and client-reported sales direction to refine future creative priorities.

The client reported that daily sales grew from approximately 100 to more than 5,000 across several products as the brand, retail availability, content, media, and creator activity scaled together.

Evidence note: The sales result was shared directly by the client. Purisorn did not own or retain the underlying sales dataset, so the case presents it as client-reported rather than independently verified attribution.
