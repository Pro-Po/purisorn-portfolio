Challenge

A new entrant had to earn trust before it could earn clicks.

Fastwork expanded from Thailand into Indonesia, where established freelance platforms already owned awareness and high-intent searches. Paid search was competitive and costly, so sustainable growth required a local organic acquisition engine.

The work was not simply translation. The site needed Indonesian search intent, credible local references, and enough content depth to compete across a large marketplace taxonomy.

My ownership

I set the search strategy, ran paid acquisition, and led execution across two countries.

Owned directly

Keyword strategy, content roadmap, Google Ads management, performance reporting, and weekly decision-making from Thailand.

Directed

Led the Indonesian marketing team on local authority building and partner placements while coordinating with the Thai development team.

Quality controlled

Reviewed link relevance, domain authority, and quality before placements supported the wider organic growth plan.

Strategy

Build authority and search coverage together.

Local intent

Optimize for how Indonesian buyers search

Aligned on-page optimization and content expansion with local terminology, service demand, and buyer intent.

Authority

Earn signals through local execution

Set the authority-building strategy while the Indonesian team proposed and secured paid or reciprocal placements. I reviewed domain quality, relevance, and DR before approval.

Scale

Expand useful marketplace entry points

Scaled content around priority services so organic discovery could grow beyond the homepage and brand searches.

Operating model

Use SEM immediately while SEO compounds

Managed Google Ads from Thailand to bring buyers and sellers into the new marketplace while organic authority and content coverage developed.
